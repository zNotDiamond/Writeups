#TryHackMe

In what year was the Unified Kill Chain framework released?

Answer: `2017`

According to the Unified Kill Chain, how many phases are there to an attack?

Answer: `18`

What is the name of the attack phase where an attacker employs techniques to evade detection?

Answer: `Defense Evasion`

What is the name of the attack phase where an attacker employs techniques to remove data from a network?

Answer: `Exfiltration`

What phase of the Unified Kill Chain is this? (Persistence)

Answer: `Persistence`

What phase of the Unified Kill Chain is this? (Command & Control)

Answer: `Command & Control`

What phase of the Unified Kill Chain is this? (Pivoting)

Answer: `Pivoting`

What phase of the Unified Kill Chain is this? (Action on Objectives)

Answer: `Action on Objectives`

Published by Paul Pols in 2017, it expands the attack lifecycle into 18 phases grouped under three goals (In, Through, and Out).
